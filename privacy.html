Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

This policy explains what information Yellonia collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers three services, called "the Services" below:

  • The website, yellonia.net, including Yellonia News and the Emergency Services (DES) back-end
  • The wiki, wiki.yellonia.net
  • Centurion, Yellonia's Discord bot, and its connection to our Roblox groups and games

Yellonia is a community project run by volunteers. It is not affiliated with Roblox Corporation or Discord Inc. Roblox and Discord have their own privacy policies, which apply when you use their platforms.

See also our Terms of Service.

1. What we collect

Discord account information

When you use Centurion, or log in to the website with Discord, we receive your Discord user ID, username, display name and avatar. The website asks Discord only for this basic profile. It does not see your email address, your servers or your messages.

Centurion also sees the roles you hold in Yellonia's Discord servers and the commands you use. It reads messages in Yellonia's servers to count activity for XP and to register event announcements, but it does not store the content of ordinary chat messages.

Roblox account information

We store your Roblox user ID, username and rank in Yellonia's Roblox groups. To match your Discord account to your Roblox account, Centurion looks up the link you created with RoWifi.

Activity in Yellonia's systems

Centurion keeps records it needs to run the community, including:

  • XP, event announcements and RSVPs, and weekly hosting quotas and strikes
  • Leaves of absence, including the reason you give
  • Rank changes, suspensions, group bans and who made them
  • Military recruitment tickets: your branch, status, assigned officer, and the messages passed between you and your officer. When a ticket closes, a transcript is saved to a private staff channel.

Yellonia News

If you register an outlet or write for one, we store the outlet's details, the articles and images you upload, your Discord ID and display name as the author, and the Ministry of Commerce's review decisions and notes.

Emergency Services (DES)

  • Applications: your Discord ID and name, Roblox username and ID, chosen department, and your answers to the application questions.
  • Members: your Badge ID, department, rank, unit and roles, plus login times and failed login attempts. Your password is stored only as a secure hash; nobody, including staff, can read it.
  • Reports and complaints: what you write, and your Discord ID and name if you submit one while logged in.
  • Roleplay records: the CAD, criminal database, warrants and wanted list describe in-game characters and in-game offences only. They are linked to Roblox accounts, not real people. Do not enter real-world personal information into them.

Economy

We store your Roblox ID, your brokerage balance, the shares you hold, corporations you register, and a history of your trades, deposits and withdrawals.

The wiki

If you create a wiki account, we store your username, a hashed password and, if you choose to add one, your email address. The wiki records every edit you make, and your IP address is logged with your edits.

Technical information

Like most websites, our hosting providers receive your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, and keep short-term server logs. The website loads its font from Google Fonts, so your browser also connects to Google. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

2. How we use it

  • To run the Services: ranking, events, recruitment, news, DES tools, the economy and the wiki
  • To log you in and keep you logged in
  • To moderate the community, enforce our Terms of Service, and prevent abuse
  • To keep the game, website and Discord in sync, so a trade or rank change counts everywhere
  • To fix problems and keep the Services secure

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. What is public

Some things are meant to be seen by everyone:

  • Published news articles, with the author's name and outlet
  • Wiki pages and their full edit history, including your wiki username. If you edit the wiki while logged out, your IP address is shown publicly instead.
  • Parliament seats, bills and laws, and the government structure
  • The public Most Wanted list, which shows in-game characters and Roblox usernames
  • Your Roblox group rank, which Roblox already shows publicly

Everything else is visible only to you and to Yellonia staff whose role requires it. For example, recruitment tickets are seen by recruitment officers, and DES records only by DES members with the right permissions.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that make Yellonia work:

  • Discord, for logins and everything Centurion does in Discord
  • Roblox, for group ranks, bans, join requests and our games
  • RoWifi, to look up which Roblox account is linked to your Discord account
  • Vercel, which hosts the website
  • Railway, which hosts Centurion, its database and the wiki
  • Google Fonts, which serves the website's font

We may also share information if we are legally required to, or to protect someone's safety.

5. Cookies

The website uses only the cookies it needs to log you in:

  • yn_session keeps you logged in with Discord for up to 7 days.
  • yn_oauth_state protects the Discord login and is deleted after 10 minutes.
  • des_session keeps DES members logged in to the back-end.

The wiki uses its own cookies to keep you logged in and remember your settings. Neither site uses tracking or advertising cookies.

6. How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as it is useful for running the community. Some records are kept after you leave, because they are needed for moderation or for the record:

  • Ranking, suspension and ban history
  • Recruitment transcripts
  • Former DES personnel records, which can no longer be used to log in
  • Wiki edit history, which is part of each page's history

Login cookies expire on their own. Server logs kept by our hosting providers are deleted on their own schedules.

7. Your choices

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is wrong
  • Delete your information

To make a request, contact us as described in Contact. We may need to confirm your Discord or Roblox account first. We may keep some records where they are needed for moderation, such as a ban that is still in force. On the wiki, we can remove your account details, but edits stay in page history under an anonymized name.

You can also log out of the website at any time, unlink your accounts in RoWifi, or stop using the Services.

8. Age requirement

You must be at least 13 years old, or the minimum age required in your country, to use the Services. This matches Discord's own requirement. If you believe someone under 13 has given us their information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. Login cookies are signed so they cannot be faked, and the connection between the website and Centurion is protected by a secret key. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Yellonia changes. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top. If the change is significant, we will also announce it in the Yellonia Discord server.

11. Contact

For questions or requests about your information, contact a staff member in the Yellonia Discord server.
