What is Yellonia?

Yellonia is a military simulation nation on Roblox. Enlist in the Army, Navy, Air Force, Special Forces or Military Police and serve in a living capital city, in a nation run by its own members through an Emperor, a Parliament and a Supreme Court. Out of uniform, there are player-run corporations, a stock market and housing.
