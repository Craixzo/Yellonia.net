How Yellonia is governed

Three branches, one Constitution

Executive The Emperor and Cabinet Leads the Empire and runs the six ministries day to day. Legislative Parliament 80 seats. Writes and votes on the laws of the Empire. Judicial Imperial Supreme Court Five judges who interpret the laws and the Constitution.

The Emperor

The head of state and Commander in Chief of the Armed Forces. The Emperor signs or rejects the laws Parliament passes, and directs changes to government that don't require a law.

The Viceroys

The Viceroys stand beside the Emperor. If the Emperor steps down or is unavailable, they choose the next Emperor from among themselves.

The Imperial Cabinet

The Cabinet runs the ministries and carries out the work of government day to day. It is chaired by the Chancellor.

Members of the Cabinet

Parliament

Parliament writes and votes on the laws of the Empire, so that citizens have a say in how the nation is run. It is chaired by the Parliament Speaker. The chamber has 80 seats, of which 17 are currently open to be held.

    The Imperial Supreme Court

    The Court interprets the laws and the Constitution. It has five judges, each put forward by the Chancellor and confirmed by the Emperor.
