How it works

Nothing to install

F8 saves the last 30 seconds of the game as a 3D clip. Open the recorder in a browser tab and your clips get real video too.

Live, with a delay

Officers can show an event live. Viewers are a little behind the game, and nobody can watch a server they're playing in.

Display names only

No chat, no voice, no usernames. Clips show display names, teams, movement, shots and kills.

Kept for 30 days

Clips are deleted after 30 days. Want one removed sooner? Ask through Contact Staff.
